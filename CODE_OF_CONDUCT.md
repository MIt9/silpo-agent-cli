# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. No harassment, insults, or personal attacks in issues, PRs,
or discussions.

Disagree about code, not people — critique the change, not the person who
made it.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be filed as a GitHub issue, or emailed
to the maintainer at the address in [pyproject.toml](pyproject.toml).
Reports will be reviewed and handled at the maintainer's discretion,
including warning or blocking repeat offenders.
